## Catalog policy

These records are an initial curated sample, not an exhaustive or price-verified market ranking. Users should check licensing, prices, compatibility and other current terms on original sites. Research snippets are summaries created for this prototype, not copied scholarly abstracts. All links open to their official original sources.

## Pending original Phase I–IV integration

The Phase III corpus consists of unreviewed authored records, including 122 glossary candidates, essays and technical dossiers. They are not automatically imported into public routes. Before publication, a qualified editor must check source evidence, licensing, language, version currency and reviewed status.
