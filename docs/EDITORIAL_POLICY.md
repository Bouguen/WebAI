# WebAI — editorial guardrails (prelaunch)

## Status labels
- \`brouillon\`: text in preparation.
- \`a_verifier\`: written but not independently approved.
- \`valide\`: reviewed and attributed to an accountable editor, with sources, licensing and date.
- \`a_actualiser\`: previously validated; re-review needed.

## Publication gate
The build must not automatically publish \`brouillon\`, \`a_verifier\`, or \`a_actualiser\` records. Do not label a source verified merely because JSON syntax passes validation, a URL exists or a summarizing model generated a citation. Apply source-level checks, verify dates and distinguish preprint from peer-reviewed research.

## AI transparency
Clearly separate demonstrations and deterministic simulations from connected AI features. Provide visible attribution, provenance, update date and limitations for substantive published articles. Preserve human oversight for safety, copyright and legal/regulatory topics.

## Non-affiliation
WebAI is an independent educational portal. It is not the W3C's Web & AI Interest Group and is not endorsed by the W3C, MCP, A2A or other technologies discussed.

## This branch
This first build is an interface **prototype** with short editorial introduction text and links to primary documents. It deliberately does not expose the unreviewed Phase III corpus as completed reference articles.
